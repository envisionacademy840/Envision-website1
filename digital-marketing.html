Why Learn Digital Marketing?

Businesses use many digital channels to reach audiences, promote products, generate enquiries and build relationships. Digital marketing brings these together through strategy, creative execution, advertising, measurement and optimisation.

What You Can Explore

  • Digital Marketing Fundamentals

    Major channels, the customer journey and how objectives shape campaign decisions.

  • Social Media Marketing

    Platforms, content planning, audience engagement, brand communication and calendars.

  • Search Engine Optimisation

    How search engines organise information, keyword research and organic visibility.

  • Paid Advertising

    Google Ads and Meta Ads basics: objectives, audiences, creatives and budgeting.

  • Content Marketing

    Useful content, copy and messaging for awareness, engagement and lead generation.

  • Website and Landing Pages

    How pages support campaigns and guide visitors towards meaningful actions.

  • Analytics and Measurement

    Impressions, clicks, leads, conversion rates, cost per lead and other metrics.

  • Conversion Tracking

    Tools such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, where included in the final curriculum.

  • Campaign Improvement

    Reviewing results, spotting performance gaps and deciding what to improve.

  • Professional Development

    Presenting campaign findings and preparing for potential marketing roles.

Topics are indicative. Request the latest syllabus to confirm modules and tools.
