What Is Human Resources?

HR is the organisational function that manages the employee lifecycle and supports workplace operations: recruitment, onboarding, employee records, policies, training and performance processes.

What the Program May Cover

  • HR Fundamentals

    The purpose of HR and its role within an organisation.

  • Recruitment and Selection

    Candidate screening, interview coordination and selection basics.

  • Employee Onboarding

    How new employees are introduced to roles, policies and expectations.

  • HR Administration

    Employee documentation and record management, where included.

  • Communication and Conduct

    Workplace communication, confidentiality and professional behaviour.

  • Employee Development

    Training, development and performance process fundamentals.
