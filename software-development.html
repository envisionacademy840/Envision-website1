What Is Software Development?

It is the planning, creating, testing and maintaining of software applications, combining logical thinking, programming concepts, problem-solving and an understanding of how digital systems work together.

  • Web Development

  • Databases

    How applications organise, store and retrieve data, where included.

  • Problem-Solving

    A structured approach to technical problems and possible solutions.

  • Testing and Debugging

    How developers find issues, test functionality and improve reliability.

  • Project-Based Learning

    Practical exercises or projects, once confirmed by the academy.
