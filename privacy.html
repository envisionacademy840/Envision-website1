Privacy Policy

Envision Academy respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains what we may collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we may use it, and your choices.

Information We May Collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The course or service you enquire about
  • Information you voluntarily include in a message
  • Basic website usage information, such as pages visited and interactions
  • Technical information from analytics or advertising tools, where enabled

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through general enquiry forms.

How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to course enquiries and share syllabus, eligibility, fee and batch information
  • Follow up on requests you make
  • Improve our website and enquiry experience
  • Understand website performance and marketing activity, where appropriate
  • Maintain website security and meet applicable obligations

Cookies, Analytics and Advertising Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand activity and evaluate marketing performance. Depending on the tools installed, these may include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tracking or the LinkedIn Insight Tag. They may collect information about page visits, interactions or advertising activity according to their own settings and policies.

Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information collected through our enquiry forms. Service providers that help us run the website, manage enquiries, measure performance or deliver communications may process it. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legitimate rights and security.

Data Retention and Security

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law. We take appropriate steps to protect it, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about information you submitted or request an update or deletion where applicable. You may also manage cookies through your browser or any consent controls on the website.

Third-Party Websites

We may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
